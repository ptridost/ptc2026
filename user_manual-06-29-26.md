# Participant

---

## 1. Registering for the Congress

1. Navigate to the congress website and click **Register**.
2. Fill in your **Personal Information**:
   - First Name, Middle Initial, Last Name
   - Sex, Birth Date, and Age Range
   - Nationality, Country, Region / State, and City / Province
   - Institution and Position
3. Under **Profession**, check all professions that apply to you.
   - If you hold a PRC license for a selected profession, enter your license number in the field that appears below it.
   - If your profession is not listed, check **Others** and type your profession in the field provided.
4. Under **Account Details**, enter your **email address** — this will be your login username.
5. Enter and confirm your **password** (minimum 8 characters).
6. Complete the **reCAPTCHA** verification.
7. Check the box to agree to the **Data Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Use**.
8. Click **Register**.
9. A success message will appear and you will be redirected to the login page.

> **Note:** If you see an error saying the email is already taken, your email may already be registered. Try logging in instead.

> **Note:** Make sure your selected Age Range matches your actual birthdate. The system will flag a mismatch before submission.

---

## 2. Logging In

1. Navigate to the congress website and click **Login**.
2. Enter your registered **email address** and **password**.
3. Click **Log In**.
4. You will be redirected to your **Participant Dashboard**.

---

## 3. Navigating the Dashboard

Once logged in, your dashboard is divided into the following sections:

- **Profile Card** — displays your name, profession(s), PRC license number(s), and CPD units earned. Action buttons for editing your profile, certificates released, and viewing attendance are found here.
- **Today's Sessions** — shows any sessions scheduled for the current date.
- **Upcoming Sessions** — shows all sessions scheduled for future dates.
- **All Sessions** — shows the full list of all sessions. Use the **All / 2025 / 2024 / 2023** buttons at the top right to filter by year.

---

## 4. Editing Your Profile

1. On your dashboard, click **Edit Profile** in your profile card.
2. An **Edit Profile** form will appear.
3. Update any of the following fields as needed:
   - First Name, Middle Name, Last Name
   - Age Group
   - Institution
   - Email / Username
4. For the **Profession** section, refer to [Section 5](#5-adding-or-updating-your-prc-license-number).
5. Click **Save Changes**.
6. A confirmation message will appear once your profile has been updated.

> **Note:** Changing your email will also change your login username.

---

## 5. Adding or Updating Your PRC License Number

Your PRC license number is required to generate a Certificate of Attendance.

1. On your dashboard, click **Edit Profile**.
2. Scroll down to the **Profession** section.
3. Check the box next to your profession — a license number field will appear below it.
4. Enter your **PRC License Number** in the field.
5. If your profession is not listed, check **Others**, type your profession name, and enter your license number if applicable.
6. Click **Save Changes**.

> **Note:** A PRC license number is mandatory for each checked profession before the form can be submitted.

---

## 6. Viewing Sessions

### Today's Sessions

Sessions scheduled for the current date appear automatically at the top of your dashboard under **Today's Sessions**.

### Upcoming Sessions

All future sessions are listed under **Upcoming Sessions**.

### All Sessions

The **All Sessions** section shows every session from all years. To filter:

1. Scroll down to the **All Sessions** section.
2. Click **2025**, **2024**, or **2023** to show only sessions from that year.
3. Click **All** to show every session again.

---

## 7. Viewing Your Attendance

1. On your dashboard, click **View my Attendance** in your profile card.
2. You will be taken to the **My Attendance** page.
3. Each session for the selected year is shown as a card displaying:
   - The session name and date
   - **Attendance status** — PRESENT (with submission time), ABSENT, or UPCOMING
   - **Feedback status** — SUBMITTED (with submission time) or NOT SUBMITTED
4. To view attendance for a different year, click the corresponding year button at the top of the page (e.g., **2025**, **2024**, **2023**).
5. To return to the dashboard, click **Back to Dashboard**.

> **Note:** This feature is available for PTC participants only.

---

## 8. Downloading Your Certificate

1. On your dashboard, go to the **Certificates** section inside your profile card.
2. Locate the **Generate Certificate for [Year]** button for the year you want.
3. If you are eligible, the button will be active — click it to open your certificate, containing your CPD points if applicable, as a PDF in a new tab.
4. Verify if your certificate contents are correct
5. In the PDF viewer, click the **Download** icon to save your certificate.

### If the Button is Greyed Out

Click the greyed-out button — a message will appear explaining why your certificate is not yet available. Common reasons include:

- You have not met the required session attendance for that year.
- No PRC license number is on file. Click **Edit Profile** to add one.
- The certificate for that year has not been released yet — the message will show the release date.
- You have not submitted feedback at the end of each session (required for 2025 certificates).

---

## 9. Certificate Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible to generate a Certificate of Attendance, all of the following must be met:

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| **Session Attendance** | You must have attended at least **6 sessions** in the certificate year |
| **PRC License** | At least one PRC license number must be saved on your profile |
| **Release Date** | The certificate release date for that year must have passed |
| **Feedback (2025 only)** | You must have submitted feedback at the end of your attended sessions |

> **Tip:** If you are missing a PRC license number, click **Edit Profile** directly from the error message modal to add it immediately.

# Admin

---

## 10. Logging In as Admin

1. Navigate to the login page.
2. Enter the supplied **admin credentials**.
3. Verify reCAPTCHA.
4. Click **Log In**.
5. You will be redirected to the **Admin Dashboard**.

---

## 11. Navigating the Admin Dashboard

The Admin Dashboard is divided into the following sections:

- **PTC Controls** — buttons for all major admin actions (Add Session, Add Speaker, Search Participants, etc.).
- **Live Now** — shows any session currently in progress based on today's date and time.
- **Upcoming Sessions** — shows all sessions scheduled for future dates.
- **All Sessions** — shows every session across all years, filterable by year using the **All / 2025 / 2024 / 2023** buttons.
- **Dashboard Filters** — at the top of the page, used to filter the analytics charts by year and session.

Each session appears as a card with the following action buttons:

| Button | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Edit Info** | Edit the session details (date, time, title, logo, etc.) |
| **Edit Speakers** | Add or edit speakers linked to this session |
| **Edit Feedback Deadline** | Override the default feedback closing time for this session |
| **Attendance List** | View the list of participants who attended this session |
| **View Feedback** | View feedback responses submitted for this session |
| **Summary Report** | View a summary report for this session |
| **Recorded Session** | Upload or manage the recorded video for this session |
| **Delete Session** | Permanently delete this session and all its related data |

---

## 12. Viewing Analytics and Charts

Charts are hidden by default to keep the dashboard clean.

1. Click **Show Charts** at the top right of the Admin Dashboard header.
2. The charts section will expand, showing the following graphs:
   - Total Registrants / Session Attendees count
   - Gender breakdown
   - Age Demographics
   - How Participants Learned about the event
   - Motivation for Attending
   - Profession
   - Location
3. To filter all charts by year, click **2023**, **2024**, or **2025** in the **Dashboard Filters** bar.
4. To filter by a specific session, use the **session selector dropdown** in the Dashboard Filters bar. Use the **left and right arrow buttons** to cycle through sessions one by one.
5. To hide the charts again, click **Hide Charts**.

---

## 13. Adding a Session

1. In the **PTC Controls** section, click **Add Session**.
2. Fill in the session details:
   - **Date** — the date of the session
   - **Time** — the session start time
   - **End Time** — the session end time
   - **Header** — the session identifier (e.g., "Session 1")
   - **Title** — the full topic or title of the session
   - **Zoom Link** — the meeting link for the session (optional)
   - **CPD Units** — the number of CPD units for the session (optional)
   - **Logo Image Filename** — type the filename of an existing logo image (optional)
   - **Or Upload New Logo** — upload a new image file directly (jpg, jpeg, png, gif only)
3. Click **ADD**.
4. A success message will appear and the new session will be visible on the dashboard.

> **Note:** The time fields default to a morning (9:00 AM – 12:00 PM) or afternoon (1:00 PM – 5:00 PM) range depending on the time of day you open the form.

---

## 14. Editing a Session

1. Locate the session you want to edit under **Upcoming Sessions** or **All Sessions**.
2. Click **Edit Info** on the session card.
3. The **Edit Session** form will appear, pre-filled with the current session details.
4. Update any of the following fields:
   - Date, Time, End Time
   - Header, Title, Link
   - CPD Units
   - Logo — either type a new filename or upload a new image file
5. A logo preview is shown on the right side of the form to confirm the current image.
6. Click **Save Changes**.
7. A success message will appear confirming the update.

> **Note:** If you upload a new logo image, the old logo file will be automatically deleted from the server.

---

## 15. Deleting a Session

1. Locate the session you want to delete under **Upcoming Sessions** or **All Sessions**.
2. Click **Delete Session** on the session card.
3. A confirmation modal will appear showing the session name.
4. Read the warning carefully — this action will permanently delete the session and **all related attendance, feedback, and speaker data**.
5. Click **Yes, Permanently Delete** to confirm.
6. A success message will appear and the session will be removed from the dashboard.

> **Warning:** Deletion cannot be undone. Make sure you have exported any attendance or feedback data you need before deleting a session.

---

## 16. Adding a Speaker

1. In the **PTC Controls** section, click **Add Speaker**.
2. Fill in the speaker details:
   - **Session** — select the session this speaker belongs to from the dropdown
   - **Speaker Name** — the full name of the speaker
   - **Speaker Description** — a short bio or title
   - **Sex** — select Male, Female, or Prefer not to mention
   - **Speaker Image** — upload a photo of the speaker (jpg, jpeg, png only)
3. Click **ADD SPEAKER**.
4. A success message will appear and the speaker will be linked to the selected session.

> **Note:** A speaker image file must be selected before submitting. The form will not proceed without one.

---

## 17. Managing Speakers on a Session Card

To edit or remove speakers already linked to a specific session:

1. Locate the session on the dashboard.
2. Click **Edit Speakers** on the session card.
3. You will be taken to the **Edit Speaker Information** page for that session.
4. Make the necessary changes and save.

1. Locate the session on the dashboard.

---

## 22. Editing the Feedback Deadline

Each session has a default feedback closing time set globally in Session Settings. You can override it for a specific session here.

1. Locate the session on the dashboard.
2. Click **Edit Feedback Deadline** on the session card.
3. The **Edit Feedback Deadline** modal will appear showing the current session title.
4. To set a custom deadline, enter a date and time in the **New Override Deadline** field.
5. To remove a previously set override and revert to the default rule, check **Clear Override (Revert to Default Rule)**.
6. Click **Save Changes**.

---

## 23. Editing Session Settings

Session Settings controls the global time rules for when attendance and feedback forms open and close across all sessions.

1. In the **PTC Controls** section, click **Session Settings**.
2. You will be taken to the **Edit Overall Session Settings** page.
3. Update any of the following fields:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Accepting Time for Feedback — Morning Sessions** | The time the feedback form opens for morning sessions |
| **Accepting Time for Feedback — Afternoon Sessions** | The time the feedback form opens for afternoon sessions |
| **Closing Time for Feedback** | The time the feedback form closes for all sessions |
| **Accepting Time for Attendance — Morning Sessions** | The time the attendance form opens for morning sessions |
| **Accepting Time for Attendance — Afternoon Sessions** | The time the attendance form opens for afternoon sessions |

4. Click **Save Changes**.
5. A success message will appear and the settings will be applied globally across all sessions.

> **Note:** To override the feedback deadline for a specific session only, use the **Edit Feedback Deadline** button on the session card instead. See [Section 22](#22-editing-the-feedback-deadline).

> **Note:** Click **Cancel** to discard any changes and return to the Admin Dashboard.
